Keep any Oct 10 revision separately staged until its Oct 24 effective date; do not apply future rules early.
5. **Prepare for the Oct 20 behavior contract change.** Add a separate compatibility/test track (no live call) for per-block `decide()` timeout, strategic LLM revision timeout, cross-epoch internal state persistence (64 MiB), epoch-index/total (`s`, `k`) inputs, and the revised scoring baseline. The rules distinguish the 5-second per-block decision response from the up-to-5-minute strategy-revision call; revision runs outside the block-production critical path. They define epoch-start `V_0` from the initial endowment priced at epoch start, rather than actual pre-run holdings, and define the effective price of market-only assets by the one-sided price for selling the full evaluated quantity. If revisions are configured every 60 blocks, the rules estimate about 360 revision calls across 60×360-block epochs, so set and verify a conservative model-call/cost ceiling before any eligible test. A configured cap is not evidence of provider-enforced cap.
6. **Keep exploration inside the published scope.** The target list applies to practice and live environments. Before any exploit-oriented test, resolve the exact contract by protocol name from the fresh manifest and traverse the allowed protocol graph. Price feeds/oracles, MarketRegistry, organizer-only functions, Aave faucet/test assets, organizer wallets/infrastructure, external services, real-protocol common-code vulnerabilities, evaluation bugs, and attacks that create assets without cost/risk or interfere with another agent are out of scope. Use only gateway-allowlisted `eth_`/`net_`/`web3_` methods and locally signed transactions submitted with `eth_sendRawTransaction`; do not depend on pending/txpool visibility. The nonce-only `eth_getTransactionCount` exception is from the Discord maintenance preannouncement and still requires confirmation in the rebuilt source. If scope is uncertain, ask the organizer before acting; report out-of-scope defects instead of exploiting them.
